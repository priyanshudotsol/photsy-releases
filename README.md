# Photsy for Mac: downloads and updates

**[Photsy](https://www.photsy.app)** is a private, on-device photo organizer and search engine for Mac. It finds any photo, screenshot or screen recording on your Mac by the text inside it or by describing what's pictured.

- **Search every screenshot by its text.** On-device OCR across Desktop, Downloads, CleanShot X and Shottr folders, the Photos library and any folder, indexed in place.
- **Describe it.** Smart Search finds "dog on the beach" on Apple silicon and Intel Macs, without Apple Intelligence.
- **Also included:** faces and places, exact and look-alike duplicates, HEIC/PNG/AVIF/WebP/MOV/MP4/GIF conversion, photos to searchable PDF, and Highlights.
- **Private:** no account, nothing uploaded.

Requires macOS 15 or later. One-time license; see [pricing](https://www.photsy.app/#pricing).
